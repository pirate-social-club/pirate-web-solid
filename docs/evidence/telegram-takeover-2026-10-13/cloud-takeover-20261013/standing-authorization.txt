Standing task authorization, workspace_owner decision, 2026-10-04 local
(2026-10-03 UTC), clarified 2026-10-04: all agents have indefinite approval
for tasks opened or explicitly confirmed by the workspace_owner in the task
register, including the implementation, repair and verification steps needed
to complete them. An agent-created record alone does not enlarge this scope.

Within that scope, approval covers local implementation, builds, tests,
preservation commits, source publication through the repository's sanctioned
path, reviewed merges, staging releases and deployments, staging configuration
and bindings, device preparation and acceptance capture, repairs, recovery and
reversible task-owned cleanup. Do not ask for repeated owner approval because
a task, agent session, reviewed package or operational window continues or
changes within this scope. Approval remains in force until the workspace_owner
revokes or narrows it.

Production deployment or mutation is excluded. Enabling Rewards or another
mechanism that distributes or transfers funds is excluded. Creating or rotating
credentials, changing their permissions, or exporting secret values outside
approved credential stores and runtime workflows is excluded. Irreversible
actions, including dropping databases, deleting protected recovery evidence,
force-pushing and destructive branch or history deletion, are excluded.
These actions require a task-specific explicit workspace_owner instruction;
they are not authorized by this standing rule. Existing production and Rewards
holds remain in force.

Routine use of existing managed credentials through established login,
diagnostic and reviewed staging binding workflows remains authorized. Do not
show credential values in chat, logs or evidence. Missing administrator access
is a practical blocker to resolve with the workspace_owner; standing approval
does not manufacture credentials or grant account permissions.

Where this file, child AGENTS.md files or task records require explicit human
authorization, this recorded owner instruction supplies that authorization
only for the in-scope actions above. It supersedes separate or renewed owner
approval requirements for those actions, including dated owner approval
windows. Excluded actions retain their explicit owner authorization requirement.
Repository publication paths and technical restrictions remain unchanged.

Coordinators and reviewers may establish or renew finite execution, decision
and recovery bounds under this standing authorization. Expired evidence and
callbacks must still be refreshed and reviewed before execution. Independent
review, exact source and package checks, acceptance criteria, resource ownership
and handback, credential and inventory validity, and cleanup verification remain
required. Resolve those gates without another owner permission question.
Do not race an active writer or silently discard protected evidence. Ask for
missing information, a genuinely unclear scope or physical device readiness
when needed. External communication follows the task's explicit instructions.

Review this policy on 2026-11-04. The workspace coordinator records whether its
scope still matches the owner-confirmed task register. This is a governance
review date, not an expiry or a requirement to seek renewed approval. Changing
the approved scope requires an actual workspace_owner instruction.

When the user has authorized publication and no human reviewer is participating,
an authorized coordinator agent may perform the independent PR review and merge
after the repository's required checks are green. Record the reviewed scope,
commit SHA, checks, and merge rationale in the task file. This repository-review
exception does not waive any separate domain, safety, language-qualified, or
content-review gate defined by the task or its policy documents.
