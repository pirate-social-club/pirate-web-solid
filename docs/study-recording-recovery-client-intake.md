The UI consumes the standalone api-next client 0.119.0 for its optional
StudySessionV2 spoken_submission projection. Current ordinary session reads
provide null or the exact blocked card and attempt; historical snapshots may
omit the field. The browser preserves that authority and never clears an unknown
provider outcome or invents a completed or abandoned lesson.

The UI inspects structured short-recording, definite-refusal and unknown-outcome
errors before generic conflict recovery. Only the first two permit a new
recording. Unknown outcomes pause recording and show the same state after a
fresh session read. The one-second browser floor validates reported elapsed
capture time; it does not establish decoded audio duration or a provider minimum.
