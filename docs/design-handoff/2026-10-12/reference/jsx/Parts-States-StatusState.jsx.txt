          Under review. The block that says where something stands: a mark, a heading, an explanation, and what can be done. It shares spacing and order, not length. A simple state is a heading and a line; a recovery state may carry evidence, a deadline and two actions, and that is right when both actions are real.<br />Order, top to bottom: mark (40px), 16px, heading (20px, semibold), 8px, explanation (16px, muted), then any evidence as rows (Parts/Rows/ReviewRows), then any deadline as its own line, 24px, then actions, the main one first.<br />Six states that must stay apart, by mark and by words, never by colour alone.<br />Waiting: we are doing something or watching for something. A ring. It never looks finished.<br />Uncertain: we cannot tell what happened, such as a wallet send with no answer. A question mark. It says what is being checked and never guesses.<br />Held: nothing is moving; something is kept safe until a condition is met. A pause mark. A hold can have a problem behind it, so the block says why when it is known.<br />Recoverable: something needs the person to act, and acting can still fix this same attempt. An exclamation mark. It carries the action, and any evidence or deadline that bears on it. It is never worded as a failure.<br />Failed: this attempt ended. A cross. It says so without claiming more than is known, and it may offer a way on, including starting another valid attempt.<br />Done: it worked. A tick.<br />"Sent" and "Paid" are both Done and are different facts: Sent is an outgoing transfer, Paid is a confirmed reward payment. Sharing this layout does not make the words interchangeable. Nothing is called Paid before the payment is confirmed.<br />Two forms. The block form above is for a dialog, a sheet or a whole screen.
