# Original-audio video composer

This evidence reconciles the reviewed video-post frame with the ratified
phase-one contract. The Storybook surface is presentational. It opens no camera,
records no media, uploads no bytes, and calls no API.

## Phase-one flow

The entry probes capture capability before exposing the shutter. A compatible
mobile browser gets a 9:16 camera surface with upload and camera-flip actions. A
denied camera retains upload. A browser without usable H.264 and AAC capture
receives an unavailable explanation before recording and may choose a compatible
MP4 or MOV instead. Desktop offers upload only in the same 9:16 frame.

Rotation ends the current take with a retake explanation because physical-device
evidence showed that a mid-recording sample-size change prevents fragmented-MP4
finalization. Backgrounding by itself does not end a take.

Review contains the video preview and one optional caption. It has no title,
description, trim, guide song, price, licence selector, royalty editor, or
client-selected poster. Settings state that the source is original audio, the
server generates the poster, and soundtrack rights are checked before
publication.

Publication distinguishes upload, processing, a known-recording rejection,
soundtrack review, visual or caption moderation, retryable failure, and published
playback-pending. Held operations remain private. Retry retains the same attempt;
playback preparation does not ask for another upload.

## Feed reconciliation

The existing Shorts feed designs already provide the 9:16 playback destination
and action rail. This composer does not redesign either feed. Its preview keeps
the same 9:16 media priority and treats the caption as supporting copy rather
than a title card. Playback-pending is typed honestly instead of inserting an
unplayable item into the feed.

## Deferred work

Song selection, guide playback, clip alignment, soundtrack replacement, WebM
ingest, server rendering, and the sealed master identity remain phase two. They
are absent rather than disabled.

The standalone video Post page still has no reviewed design evidence. Its route,
playback chrome, caption placement, original-sound navigation, engagement, and
playback-pending presentation require a separate design record before product
wiring.
